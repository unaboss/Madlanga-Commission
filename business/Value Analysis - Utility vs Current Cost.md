# Value Analysis: Utility Provided vs Current Cost

**Commission Research Intelligence Dashboard**

---

## 1. Overview

The Commission Research Intelligence Dashboard compresses document-processing time so the legal team spends less time on paperwork and more time on investigative thinking.

**The commission doesn't need more time — the value of the existing time needs to be improved.**

This document measures how much reading, searching, cross-referencing, and comparing the dashboard eliminates, and translates that into time recovered and cost avoided. It then maps the dashboard's pricing against the hearing days and budget it protects.

The dashboard does not replace a single evidence leader, junior researcher, or commissioner. Every legal and investigative decision stays with the advocates. What it does is eliminate the document-processing delay between a question and its answer — turning weeks of manual reading into seconds of search.

---

## 2. The Commission's Problems

The Madlanga Commission's legal team works with:

- **14,700+ pages of raw PDF transcripts** across 127 hearing days — no single person can read and remember all of it
- **Manual cross-referencing** — finding which witnesses mentioned which entities requires re-reading or keyword-searching individual PDFs
- **23.8 million characters of transcript text** — approximately 265 hours of reading per person to get through the full record
- **53 formal documents plus exhibits** — scattered rulings, statements, applications, and media statements
- **No entity tracking** — 28,705 entity mentions extracted across the record, but no way to instantly see them all without a tool
- **No network analysis** — connections between witnesses and the entities they name exist only in the heads of the evidence leaders

**The result:** Legal researchers spend hours doing what a computer can do in milliseconds. At blended daily rates of R212,000–R432,000 for the full legal team, every hour lost to document-friction is time that cannot be spent on examination preparation, cross-referencing, or building the evidentiary record.

---

## 3. How We Measure Time Recovery

Our metric is **document-processing volume recovered** — how much reading, searching, and cross-referencing the dashboard eliminates from the legal team's workflow.

For each utility, we calculate:

1. **Manual time required** — hours a person would need to do this task manually across the full commission record
2. **Dashboard time required** — seconds or minutes the dashboard takes for the same task
3. **Time recovered** — manual time minus dashboard time = hours returned to the legal team for higher-value work

All calculations use the commission's own published figures:
- Blended legal team hourly cost: **R5,300 – R10,800/hour** (derived from State Attorney daily scales)
- Full legal team daily cost: **R212,000 – R432,000/day**
- Transcript processing speed: **20 pages/hour** for careful reading and entity tagging
- Average transcript segments per full hearing day: **1,080 segments**
