## Utility 1: Entity Extraction & Cross-Referencing

### What the Dashboard Does

Automatically reads every transcript, statement, and document, extracts every named entity (people, organizations, locations, money amounts, case references, dates, exhibits), and cross-references them across the entire record. 100,000+ entity mentions from 147+ hearing days, instantly searchable.

### Manual Equivalent Cost

The manual alternative would be a junior legal researcher reading every transcript page-by-page and manually recording every entity mention into a spreadsheet or database.

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Transcript pages to process | ~14,700 (147 days × ~100 pages) |
| Careful reading + entity tagging speed | ~20 pages/hour |
| Total manual hours | ~735 hours |
| Person-days required (one-off) | ~92 days |
| **One-off manual cost (junior rate)** | **R644,000 – R1,380,000** |
| Ongoing per new hearing day | ~5 hours/day (R4,375 – R9,375/day) |
| Ongoing cost over 60 remaining hearing days | R262,500 – R562,500 |

### Value Provided

| Component | Value |
|---|---|
| One-off retrospective extraction (already done) | R644,000 – R1,380,000 |
| Ongoing extraction through Nov 2026 | R262,500 – R562,500 |
| **Total** | **R906,500 – R1,942,500** |

## Utility 2: Full-Text Search Across All Records

### What the Dashboard Does

Unified full-text search across all transcripts, rulings, statements, and documents from a single interface. Supports keywords, exact phrases, wildcards, and boolean operators with date and speaker filters. Results include surrounding context with highlights.

### Manual Equivalent Cost

Every time an evidence leader needs to find something in the record — a prior statement, a specific date, a document reference — they either:
- Remember it (unreliable across 147 days)
- Search individual PDFs one at a time (1–2 hours per search)
- Ask a junior researcher to find it (2–4 hours)

### What the Commission Currently Spends on Search

| Item | Per Day | Over 60 Remaining Hearing Days |
|---|---|---|
| Searches performed per day (conservative) | 5–10 | 300–600 |
| Manual time per search | 1–2 hours | — |
| Total manual search time per day | 5–20 hours | — |
| Blended hourly cost (across team) | R5,300 – R10,800/hour | — |
| **Daily search cost (manual)** | **R26,500 – R54,000** | — |
| **Total search cost over remaining period** | — | **R1,590,000 – R3,240,000** |

### Value Provided

The dashboard eliminates this entire cost. **Value: R1,590,000 – R3,240,000** over the remaining commission period.

## Utility 4: Evidence Compare (Documents vs Testimony)

### What the Dashboard Does

Select any entity and see in parallel what the commission's documents say about it versus what witnesses said. Exposes gaps, contradictions, and corroborations instantly.

### Manual Equivalent Cost

Comparing what commission documents say about an entity against what every witness said, across the full record:

| Activity | Time per Entity | Cost per Entity |
|---|---|---|
| Finding all document references to entity | 1–3 hours | R2,500 – R15,000 |
| Finding all testimony references to entity | 2–4 hours | R5,000 – R20,000 |
| Comparing, identifying gaps/contradictions | 2–4 hours (senior) | R10,000 – R32,000 |
| **Total per entity** | **5–11 hours** | **R17,500 – R67,000** |

With 50+ key entities in the commission record, the manual cost of comprehensive evidence comparison would be **R875,000 – R3,350,000**.

### Value Provided

The dashboard does this instantly at no marginal cost. **Value: R875,000 – R3,350,000**.

## What This Means for Pricing

### The Dashboard Replaces R4–R10.6 Million in Manual Work

The conservative estimate (R4 million) already justifies a significant investment. The realistic estimate (R7 million+) puts the tool in a completely different category from a simple software purchase.

### What the Commission Budget Can Support

The Goods & Services budget line is R100.7 million. Even at:

- **0.5% of G&S budget** → R503,500
- **1% of G&S budget** → R1,007,000
- **2% of G&S budget** → R2,014,000

### Recommended Pricing Range

| Level | Price | Justification |
|---|---|---|
| **Minimum** | R350,000 | 0.35% of G&S budget. Below this, the negotiation effort exceeds the contract value. |
| **Fair market** | R500,000 – R750,000 | 0.5–0.75% of G&S budget. Equivalent to 8–12 days of the Chief Evidence Leader's time. Replace 12% of the manual research cost. |
| **Premium** | R750,000 – R1,200,000 | 0.75–1.2% of G&S budget. Reflects that the tool is already built, zero risk, and delivers R4–R10M in value. |

### Comparison Points for Negotiation

- **R64,000** — One day of Adv. Chaskalson SC
- **R500,000** — ~8 days of the Chief Evidence Leader
- **R750,000** — ~12 days of the Chief Evidence Leader
- **R1,000,000** — 0.99% of the Goods & Services budget
- **R4,000,000+** — Conservative estimate of the manual work the dashboard replaces

### What the Commission Does Not Have to Pay

Because the dashboard is already built:

- No development costs (R200,000 – R500,000 if built from scratch)
- No IT procurement lead time (8–16 weeks typical for government)
- No project management overhead (15–20% of project value)
- No integration costs (runs standalone)
- No training costs beyond 1 day (1 hour sufficient for most users)
