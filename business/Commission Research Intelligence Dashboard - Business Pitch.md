## 1. Executive Summary

The Commission Research Intelligence Dashboard is an operational research tool purpose-built for the Madlanga Commission's legal team. It ingests, structures, and cross-references the entire public record of the commission â€” 147+ hearing days, thousands of transcript pages, rulings, statements, and documents â€” and surfaces connections between witnesses, entities, and evidence in seconds.

By making every connection between witnesses, entities, and evidence instantly visible, the dashboard ensures that evidence leaders build a more complete, more coherent evidentiary record â€” fewer missed connections, stronger cross-examinations, and a final report that rests on every relevant piece of testimony, not just what the team can remember or manually find.

**This is not a replacement tool.** It does not reduce headcount, replace any evidence leader, or automate any judgment call. The dashboard simply eliminates the manual labour of finding, cross-referencing, and recalling information â€” giving each team member the equivalent of a research assistant with perfect memory of every word spoken across 147 hearing days. **The commission doesn't need more time â€” the value of the existing time needs to be improved.** Every hour spent reading PDFs is an hour not spent on investigative thinking. Every hearing day lost to late evidence bundles is a day the commission cannot recover. The dashboard compresses the document-processing friction that slows the investigation down â€” not by adding more people to the problem, but by making the research instant.

The tool is already built, tested, and populated with all commission data to date. It requires no development lead time, no IT procurement, and no integration with government systems. It is ready to deploy immediately.

## 3. Our Solution

The Commission Research Intelligence Dashboard is a comprehensive investigative research tool that transforms unstructured public records into an interconnected, searchable intelligence platform. Its design philosophy centers on two principles: **investigative quality** (no connection goes unseen) and **efficiency** (every search, cross-reference, and comparison happens in seconds, not hours).

### Core Capabilities

#### A. Entity Extraction & Cross-Referencing

Every transcript, statement, and document is automatically processed to extract named entities: people, organizations, locations, money amounts, case references, dates, and exhibits. Each extraction preserves the surrounding context so you can see exactly what was said.

- **147+ hearing days** â€” all transcripts parsed and entity-tagged
- **100,000+ entity mentions** â€” extracted and linked to their source
- **Instant recall** â€” type an entity name and see every mention across the entire record

#### B. Network Graph â€” Visualizing Connections

The interactive network graph maps every witness against every entity they named. Bigger nodes = more mentions. Darker nodes = more central to the record. Click any node to see what entities a witness named, or which witnesses named a specific entity.

- **Overview mode** â€” see the top 80 most-connected people and entities at a glance
- **Full network mode** â€” explore all connections with filtering by role and connection count
- **Edge details** â€” click a connection line to see the actual testimony excerpt

#### C. Full-Text Search Across the Entire Record

Search every transcript, ruling, statement, and document from a single interface. Supports keywords, exact phrases, wildcards, and boolean operators. Filter by date range and speaker.

- **Unified index** â€” all text sources in one searchable database
- **Rich snippets** â€” results show surrounding context with highlights
- **Speaker filtering** â€” narrow results to specific witnesses or evidence leaders

#### D. Timeline â€” Hearing History at a Glance

See every hearing day plotted on an interactive timeline. Click any day to see the summary. Track the commission's progress from Day 1 to Day 147+.

#### E. Entity Browser

Browse all extracted entities (16,000+ unique values) by type: organizations, people, locations, case references, money amounts, exhibits, and vehicles. Select any entity to see every context it appears in across the entire record.

#### F. Story Map â€” Parallel Timelines

Two synchronized timelines â€” commission documents on one side, witness testimony on the other â€” filtered by entity, witness, and date range. See what the commission was publishing while witnesses were testifying. Highlight cross-references to spot contradictions and corroborations.

#### G. Evidence Compare

Select any entity (person, organization, location) and see in parallel:

- **What the commission's documents say** about that entity
- **What the witnesses say** about that entity

This is the core investigative function â€” finding gaps, contradictions, and corroborations between the official record and live testimony. It directly improves the quality of the investigation by ensuring evidence leaders have a complete picture before they put a question to a witness.

## How It Fits Into the Daily Workflow

The dashboard does not require anyone to change how they work. It plugs into existing workflows at specific points where manual effort is highest:

### Before a Hearing (Evidence Preparation â€” 30 min â†’ 2 min)

**Current workflow:** An evidence leader is preparing to examine a witness tomorrow. They need to find every prior statement this witness made, every entity they named, and any contradictions with commission documents. A junior researcher spends 2â€“4 hours searching through PDFs, or the evidence leader relies on memory.

**With the dashboard:** Open the dashboard. Type the witness name in Search. Every segment of their testimony appears instantly, organized by date. Open Evidence Compare and select the key entities â€” the dashboard shows what the witness said vs what commission documents say, side by side. Total time: 2 minutes.

### During a Hearing (Real-Time Reference â€” 10 min â†’ 30 seconds)

**Current workflow:** A witness says something that contradicts a prior witness from 3 months ago. The evidence leader makes a mental note, but the moment passes â€” finding the exact excerpt during a live hearing is impractical.

**With the dashboard:** Type the entity or topic into Search while the witness is speaking. The dashboard shows every mention across the entire record with context. The evidence leader can follow up immediately, strengthening the cross-examination in real time.

### After a Hearing (Cross-Referencing & Report Prep â€” 4 hours â†’ 5 minutes)

**Current workflow:** After a hearing day, a junior researcher is asked to compile all references to a specific entity (e.g., "Medicare 24" or "Feroz Khan") across the full record â€” every document mention and every witness mention. This takes hours of manual searching.

**With the dashboard:** Open the Entity Browser. Select the entity. The dashboard shows every mention across all 147 days â€” who said it, when, and the exact context. Export for use in the final report. Time: 5 minutes.

### Building the Final Report

The dashboard's ability to instantly surface every reference to any entity across the full record means the team can write a report that rests on every relevant piece of testimony â€” not just what they can remember or manually find. The parallel Evidence Compare view ensures the report accurately reflects both the documentary record and the live testimony, side by side.

In all of these cases, the dashboard **does not make a single legal or investigative decision.** Every question, every conclusion, every recommendation remains with the evidence leaders. The dashboard simply removes the friction of finding information â€” giving the team more time to think, question, and build a stronger case.

### Journalist Workflow â€” The Dashboard as an Investigative Research Tool

The same capabilities that serve the legal team also serve journalists covering the commission. The dashboard functions as an **investigative research platform** for beat reporters, investigative journalists, and data-driven newsrooms:

- **Entity tracing across time:** A journalist following the "Big Five" cartel story can search once and see every witness who named it, every context in which it was mentioned, and every document that references it â€” across all 147 hearing days. What would take a researcher weeks of reading transcripts happens in seconds.
- **Network graph as story map:** The interactive network graph visualises connections between witnesses and entities. A journalist spots patterns no single hearing covers: Witness A in March and Witness B in June both named the same entity, but their testimony was months apart. The connection exists only in the data â€” the graph surfaces it as a story lead.
- **Contradiction detection as investigative angle:** When two witnesses give conflicting accounts about the same entity, the dashboard flags it. The journalist has a sourced story with Day:Line citations for both versions â€” no tip-off needed, just data.
- **Data-backed reporting:** Every search result includes the hearing day, page number, and speaker. Every story point is verifiable against the public record. No anonymous sources, no off-the-record briefings â€” just the transcript.
- **Beat advantage:** A journalist covering the commission full-time needs to track 100+ witnesses across 147 days across 23.8 million characters of transcript text. No human can hold that in memory. The dashboard makes it searchable, cross-referenced, and visual â€” giving the beat reporter investigative depth that would otherwise require a team of researchers.

The dashboard does not replace the journalist's judgment. It replaces the weeks of manual transcript-reading that stands between a journalist and a story. What a data journalist can do with a team of researchers and months of preparation, a single journalist can do with the dashboard and an afternoon.

### Technical Architecture

- **Language**: R/Shiny (fully portable)
- **Data storage**: SQLite (single file, no server needed)
- **NLP pipeline**: Python-based entity extraction using commission-optimized rules
- **Deployment**: Runs on any Windows or Linux machine. No internet required after initial data load.
- **Security**: All data is already public. No classified or confidential material is ingested.
