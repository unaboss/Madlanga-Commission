# Media Collaboration: Video Series & News Partnerships

**Stemming from the Commission Research Intelligence Dashboard**

---

## Overview

The Commission Research Intelligence Dashboard gives us a unique position in the Madlanga Commission media landscape. We have structured, cross-referenced data that no newsroom has — every entity mention, every witness connection, every timeline organized and searchable. This is the raw material for compelling, data-driven journalism and documentary content.

This document outlines how to package that capability into media partnerships and a direct-to-audience content operation.

---

## Asset: What We Bring

| Asset | Description | Competitor Advantage |
|---|---|---|
| **Structured data** | Every entity, witness, and document cross-referenced across 147+ hearing days | No newsroom has this — they work from transcripts and memory |
| **Network analysis** | Who said what about whom, visualized | Enables data-driven stories other outlets cannot produce |
| **Search & retrieval** | Find any connection in seconds | Speed of story development — first to break cross-referenced stories |
| **Institutional knowledge** | Deep understanding of the commission record | Can provide expert commentary, not just reporting |
| **Dashboard technology** | The tool itself is a story — "how we tracked everything" | Unique behind-the-scenes content angle |

## Partnership Model 1: SABC News (Public Broadcaster)

### Format
12-part documentary series, 26–52 minutes per episode, broadcast on SABC 2 or SABC 3.

### Value to SABC
- Commission coverage fulfills their public mandate
- Pre-packaged, researched storylines — minimal production lead time
- Exclusive access to data-driven insights no other broadcaster has
- High public interest — commission has been front-page news for a year

### Value to Us
- Broadcast reach (millions of viewers)
- Production budget (SABC commissions content from independent producers)
- Credibility and brand association with a national broadcaster
- Payment: R50,000 – R150,000 per episode (independent producer rates for SABC) = **R600,000 – R1,800,000 for 12 episodes**

### Our Role
- Research & story development (using the dashboard)
- Episode scripting and narrative design
- On-screen analyst / presenter (optional)
- Fact-checking and source verification

### SABC's Role
- Production facilities (cameras, studio, editing)
- Broadcast scheduling and promotion
- Compliance and editorial oversight

### Approach
1. Develop a **sizzle reel** (3–5 min teaser using commission footage + our analysis)
2. Pitch to SABC **Commissioning Editor — Documentaries**
3. Alternatively, approach through an independent production company with SABC relationships

## Partnership Model 2: Daily Maverick (Digital Investigative Journalism)

### Format
Ongoing investigative journalism partnership — articles, interactive features, and data-driven exclusives published on Daily Maverick's platform.

### Value to Daily Maverick
- Exclusive data-driven stories their journalists cannot replicate
- Interactive web features (embedded network graphs, entity browsers) — increases time-on-site and subscriptions
- First access to your analysis — competitive advantage in commission coverage
- Daily Maverick already covers the commission extensively (they sued over in-camera hearings)

### Value to Us
- Editorial platform with 500,000+ monthly readers
- Reputation by association (Daily Maverick is award-winning investigative journalism)
- Potential for a recurring column or "Data Watch" segment
- Payment: R5,000 – R15,000 per feature article = **R60,000 – R180,000 for 12 features** (or retainer model)

### Our Role
- Propose data-driven story angles
- Provide research, entity analysis, and network visualizations
- Write or co-author articles
- Build interactive web features using dashboard data

### Daily Maverick's Role
- Editorial review and publication
- Promotion across their channels
- Payment for content

### Approach
1. Contact **Daily Maverick Investigations Desk** or editors who covered the commission's in-camera lawsuit
2. Pitch 3–5 specific story angles with sample data from the dashboard
3. Offer an exclusive first article as a demonstration

## Partnership Model 3: YouTube Channel (Direct-to-Consumer)

### Format
Self-published 12-episode video series, 15–30 minutes per episode, uploaded weekly.

### Cost Structure

| Item | Estimated Cost |
|---|---|
| Basic production (screen recording + voiceover) | R0 – R5,000 total |
| Mid-tier (scripted, edited with b-roll and motion graphics) | R15,000 – R30,000 total |
| Full production (studio, presenter, professional editing) | R50,000 – R150,000 total |

### Revenue Potential

| Source | Estimate for 12-episode series |
|---|---|
| YouTube ad revenue (10k–50k views/episode) | R5,000 – R30,000 |
| Sponsorship / brand deal | R20,000 – R100,000 |
| YouTube memberships / Super Chat | R5,000 – R20,000 |
| **Total** | **R30,000 – R150,000** |

### Approach
1. Start lean — screen recording + voiceover using the dashboard as visual reference
2. Each episode demonstrates a specific finding from the dashboard
3. Build audience over the first 4 episodes, then introduce higher production value
4. Use episodes as portfolio to pitch SABC / Daily Maverick
