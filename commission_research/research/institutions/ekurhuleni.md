# City of Ekurhuleni Metropolitan Municipality

**Parent Institution:** Gauteng Provincial Government / COGTA  
**Type:** Category A Metropolitan Municipality  
**Status:** Active — under SCOPA scrutiny  

---

## Structure

```
National Government (COGTA, National Treasury)
  └── Gauteng Provincial Government
       └── City of Ekurhuleni
            ├── Council (political)
            │    └── Executive Mayor
            │         └── Mayoral Committee (MMCs)
            └── Administration
                 └── City Manager (Accounting Officer, PFMA)
                      ├── CFO
                      ├── HOD: Human Resources
                      ├── HOD: Legal
                      ├── HOD: Community Safety (oversees EMPD)
                      ├── HOD: Water and Sanitation
                      └── Chief of Police (EMPD)
```

**Budget authority:** City Manager is the Accounting Officer under the MFMA. Responsible for all financial management, supply chain, and audit compliance.
