# NPA — National Prosecuting Authority

**Parent Institution:** Department of Justice and Constitutional Development  
**Established:** 1998 (NPA Act, Act 32 of 1998)  
**Status:** Active

---

## Structure

```
President Ramaphosa
  └── Minister of Justice
       └── Adv Andy JL Mothibi — NDPP (National Director of Public Prosecutions)
            ├── Adv Nicolette Bell — Deputy NDPP: National Prosecutions Service
            ├── Adv Chuma Mtengwane — Deputy NDPP: Asset Forfeiture Unit
            ├── Adv Andrea Johnson — Head of IDAC (Investigating Directorate) [resigned 26 Jul 2026]
            │    ├── Adv Annemarie Friedman — Deputy Director of Public Prosecutions
            │    ├── Adv Peter Serunye — Deputy Director
            │    └── Adv Paul Louw — Senior Deputy Director
            ├── National Prosecutions Service
            ├── Specialised Commercial Crime Unit (SCCU)
            ├── Priority Crimes Litigation Unit (PCLU)
            ├── Sexual Offences and Community Affairs (SOCA)
            └── Office for Witness Protection
```

**Budget authority:** NDPP is the Accounting Officer. The NPA's budget is voted by Parliament under the Justice portfolio. IDAC's budget flows through the NPA.

### Known Issues (from public record)
- NPA has a history of **qualified audit opinions** — particularly around:
  - Irregular expenditure (especially on lease agreements, IT systems)
  - Supply chain management non-compliance
  - Vacancy rate issues (prosecutor shortages affecting performance)
- IDAC's budget is a line item within NPA; no separate IDAC audit opinion is published

## Cross-Institutional Links to Track

| Institution | Link to NPA | 
|---|---|
| **IDAC** | Directly under NPA — NDPP is accounting officer |
| **SAPS** | IDAC investigates SAPS corruption cases; tension between IDAC mandate and SAPS visible in transcripts |

| **DPCI / HAWKS** | IDAC works alongside DPCI on priority crime cases |
| **IPID** | IPID refers police misconduct cases that may overlap with IDAC's corruption mandate |
