# SAPS — South African Police Service

**Parent Institution:** Department of Police (Ministry of Police)  
**Established:** 1995 (SAPS Act, Act 68 of 1995)  
**Budget (2025/26):** R120.9 billion  
**Status:** Active

---

## Structure

```
President Ramaphosa
  └── Minister Senzo Mchunu — Minister of Police (political head)
       └── National Commissioner (Accounting Officer)
            ├── Deputy National Commissioner: Crime Detection
            ├── Deputy National Commissioner: Policing
            ├── Deputy National Commissioner: Corporate Services
            ├── Chief Financial Officer (CFO)
            ├── Divisional Commissioners
            │    ├── Crime Intelligence
            │    ├── Forensic Science Laboratory
            │    ├── Supply Chain Management
            │    └── ...
            └── Provincial Commissioners (9 provinces)
                 └── Cluster Commanders (Brigadiers)
                      └── Station Commanders (Colonels)
                           └── Captains, Warrant Officers, Sergeants
```

**Budget authority:** National Commissioner is the Accounting Officer. The Minister of Police sets policy direction.

## Cross-Institutional Links to Track

| Institution | Link to SAPS |
|---|---|
| **IDAC / NPA** | IDAC investigates SAPS corruption cases; tension between IDAC mandate and SAPS evident in transcripts (Day 139–141) |
| **IPID** | Investigates SAPS misconduct; IPID reports were discussed in testimony as often being ignored by SAPS leadership |
| **DPCI / HAWKS** | Priority crimes unit within SAPS; blurred line between regular SAPS and HAWKS |
| **SSA** | Secondments from SSA into IDAC to investigate SAPS |
| **EMPD / Ekurhuleni** | EMPD governed by SAPS Act but managed by municipality; Mashazi testified about the tension |
| **National Treasury** | Controls SAPS budget via PFMA; SAPS must report on irregular expenditure |
