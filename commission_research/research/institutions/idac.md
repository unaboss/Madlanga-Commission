# IDAC — Investigating Directorate Against Corruption

**Parent Institution:** NPA (National Prosecuting Authority)  
**Established:** 4 April 2019 (Proclamation 20 of 2019, in terms of Section 7 of the NPA Act)  
**Status:** Active (Head resigned 26 Jul 2026)

---

## Structure

```
President Ramaphosa
  └── Minister of Justice
       └── Adv Andy JL Mothibi — NDPP (National Director of Public Prosecutions)
            └── Adv Andrea Johnson — Special Director / Head of IDAC
                 ├── Adv Annemarie Friedman — Deputy Director of Public Prosecutions
                 ├── Adv Peter Serunye — Deputy Director
                 └── Adv Paul Louw — Senior Deputy Director
```

**Budget authority:** IDAC's budget is part of the NPA budget. Head of IDAC is not a standalone Accounting Officer — the NDPP is the Accounting Officer for the NPA.

## Commission Witnesses Who Testified About IDAC

### Colonel Padayachee (Days 139–141) — IDAC Investigating Officer
- Primary IDAC investigating officer
- Testified extensively about IDAC processes, docket handling, intake procedures
- Cross-examined by Adv Sello SC, Adv Khumalo SC, Adv Baloyi SC
- Responded to allegations of a "Khan-IDAC cabal"
- **Key exchange with Chair (Day 141):** Justice Madlanga asked: *"So, Colonel, IDAC has arrogated to itself the power to be an overseer over SAPS. Is that what you are saying?"* — suggesting the commission itself questioned IDAC overreach

### Adv Andrea Johnson (Day 144 — 26 Jul 2026) — Head of IDAC
- Testified as a witness under oath
- Confirmed she is "currently employed as the Investigating Director and Head of IDAC"
- Received death threats: *"I received a death threat directly on my cell phone. It read: 'Mrs [Johnson]... get body bags ready'"*
- Defended IDAC's mandate against allegations it operates outside its scope
- Addressed the arrest of Lt Gen Khumalo and surrounding controversy
- Mentioned investigations into "senior members of SAPS Crime Intelligence," including CFO Major General Solly Lazarus
- Denied existence of a "Khan-IDAC cabal"
- **Resigned same day as testimony** — asked Ramaphosa and NDPP Mothibi for permission to resign with immediate effect

- Made whistleblower-type claim: *"sources within IDAC... there are activities that are happening with IDAC that are not in line with what the purpose of [IDAC]"*

## Key Findings

1. **The commission itself questioned IDAC's mandate** — Justice Madlanga asked directly whether IDAC had "arrogated to itself the power to be an overseer over SAPS"
2. **A whistleblower early in the hearings (Day 2)** claimed IDAC was operating outside its purpose — this was dismissed then, but Johnson's resignation validates the concern
3. **Johnson resigned the same day she testified** — unusual timing suggests either:
   - Pressure from above (NDPP Mothibi / Presidency) to step down after her testimony exposed issues
   - A deal that she would resign in exchange for something
   - She was already planning to resign and the testimony was her parting statement
4. **IDAC's budget is part of the NPA** — the NDPP (Mothibi) is the Accounting Officer, meaning he ultimately controls IDAC's resources and would be responsible for any financial/audit findings
