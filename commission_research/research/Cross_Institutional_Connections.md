## 3. Investigative Threads (Connections Between Institutions)

### Thread 1: IDAC — SAPS (the most active connection)

| Evidence | Source |
|---|---|
| IDAC investigated and arrested **Lt Gen Dumisani Khumalo** (SAPS) | Day 144 testimony |
| IDAC investigated **Gen Shadrack Sibiya** (Suspended Deputy NC) | Day 139–141 testimony |
| IDAC investigating **SAPS Crime Intelligence CFO** Maj Gen Solly Lazarus | Day 144 testimony |
| Justice Madlanga asked if IDAC had "arrogated" power over SAPS | Day 141 |
| IDAC secondments from SAPS — conflict of interest | NPA website |
| Day 2 whistleblower said IDAC operating outside its mandate | Day 2 transcript |

**Implication:** The commission's own Chair questioned whether IDAC had exceeded its authority over SAPS. The head of IDAC resigned the same day she testified. This suggests the IDAC-SAPS relationship is central to understanding how anti-corruption efforts are being managed — or mismanaged.

### Thread 2: Ekurhuleni — SAPS (SAPS Act vs Municipal Systems Act)

| Evidence | Source |
|---|---|
| Mashazi's core testimony was the tension between SAPS Act governing EMPD officers and municipal delegation systems | Days 41–42 |
| Chief Mapiyeye (EMPD Chief) was disciplined under municipal processes, not SAPS processes | Days 41–42 |
| Mashazi mentioned SAPS 26 times — always in reference to the SAPS Act, never as her employer | Entity data |
| Appointment of EMPD officials followed municipal delegations, bypassing SAPS Act requirements | Days 41–42 |

**Implication:** The EMPD is a flashpoint where two legal regimes collide. This ambiguity was exploited to avoid proper accountability. The person responsible for navigating this (Mashazi) retired and was flagged by SCOPA for contracts awarded under her watch.

### Thread 3: IDAC — NPA (internal accountability failure)

| Evidence | Source |
|---|---|
| IDAC is part of NPA but investigates NPA-related corruption | NPA website |
| NDPP Mothibi controls IDAC budget and is Johnson's direct superior | NPA leadership page |
| Johnson asked both Ramaphosa AND Mothibi to resign | News24 |
| NPA failed its own audits for multiple consecutive years | PMG: Justice Portfolio (15 Oct 2025, 6 Nov 2024) |

**Implication:** The anti-corruption unit cannot independently investigate its own parent body. The NDPP who oversees IDAC is also implicated in the chain that Johnson had to go through to resign. The NPA's audit failures undermine its authority to prosecute financial crimes.

### Thread 4: Ekurhuleni — SCOPA — IDAC (contract irregularities)

| Evidence | Source |
|---|---|
| SCOPA followed up on Ekurhuleni contracts awarded during Mashazi's tenure | PMG: SCOPA (10 Jun 2026) |
| Material irregularities flagged by AGSA for Ekurhuleni | PMG: SCOPA (10 Jun 2026) |
| Lifestyle audit flagged Mashazi (London flight) | PMG: Planning, Monitoring (18 Feb 2026) |

**Implication:** The contract irregularities at Ekurhuleni could be referred to IDAC for investigation — but given IDAC's current leadership crisis (Johnson resigned) and its structural conflicts, whether this happens is uncertain.
