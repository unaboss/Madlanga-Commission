### Her Testimony Summary (Days 41–42)

**Context:** Her testimony was about her role as **City Manager of Ekurhuleni** — the administrative head and Accounting Officer. The key themes:

1. **Relationship with EMPD Chief of Police (Mapiyeye)** — She struggled to discipline the Chief of Police over sexual harassment allegations. Council and the Mayor blocked her efforts.
2. **Appointment irregularities** — Discussed the appointment of Brigadier Mkhwanazi to a deputy chief position, competency assessments not done, acting appointments exceeding 3 months.
3. **Cost containment** — She centralized attorney appointments to curtail escalating legal costs. Allegations that she did this to protect Mkhwanazi were disputed.
4. **Political interference** — The Mayor and MMC Finance pressured her to stop disciplinary processes against the Chief of Police. She refused to take "special leave."
5. **SAPS Act vs Municipal Systems Act** — Ongoing tension between the SAPS Act (governing EMPD police officers) and municipal delegation systems. She requested a policy to bridge the gap.
6. **Annexures referenced:** IM6, IM19, IM20, IM25 (documents she submitted as evidence)

**She mentions SAPS 26 times** — always in reference to the **SAPS Act** as it applies to EMPD, not as her employer.
